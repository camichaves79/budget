$5 Budget — Delete your account and data

Effective: September 2026

Delete data stored on your device

Your transactions, budgets and categories live only on your device. To erase them:

  • Open $5 Budget → Settings → Reset app (erases all transactions, budgets and categories), or
  • Uninstall the app, which removes everything stored on the device.

Request deletion of your account and server data

If you signed in with Google to buy the Smart Entry license, we store your Google account email and your purchase/subscription record (with our identity and payment providers) to deliver and restore your license. To request deletion:

  • Email info@5budget.app stating that you want your account and data deleted, and include the Google email address you used in the app.

What is deleted vs. retained

  • Deleted (within a reasonable time of your request): your license entitlement and the link to your Google account.
  • Retained: purchase/transaction records may be kept for the period required by accounting and tax law, after which they are deleted.
