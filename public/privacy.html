$5 Budget — Privacy Policy

Effective: September 2026

$5 Budget is a mobile-first personal budget tracker. Your budget data is stored entirely on your device (your browser's local storage) and is never uploaded to our servers.

What we collect

  • Google account email — when you sign in to buy or restore a license. It is used only to bind your license to your account and restore it on a new device.
  • Purchase and subscription information — when you buy the Smart Entry license (via Google Play on Android, or Lemon Squeezy on web/iOS). This includes your email, an order identifier and the amount, and is stored to verify and deliver your license and for accounting.
  • Smart-entry text — when you use AI smart entry, the text you type or dictate plus your category names are sent to our parsing service (Google Gemini) to turn it into a transaction. This text is used only to perform the parsing and is not stored by us.

What we do not collect

Your transactions, budgets and categories stay on your device. They never leave your device and are not accessible to us.

How we use data

  • To create, deliver and restore your Smart Entry license.
  • To process and record purchases for accounting and tax purposes.
  • To power the AI smart-entry parsing.

Third parties

  • Google (Firebase Auth, Google Gemini, Google Play Billing) — sign-in, AI parsing and Android payments.
  • Lemon Squeezy — merchant of record for web/iOS purchases.
  • Cloudflare — hosting.

Retention

  • License, entitlement and purchase records are kept as long as needed to provide your license and satisfy accounting and tax obligations.
  • Smart-entry text is processed transiently and not retained.

Your data on your device

You can export or erase all your local data at any time from the app's Settings. Uninstalling the app removes your local data.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer via github.com/camichaves79/budget.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
